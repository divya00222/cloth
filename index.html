Building trust,

I'm Sundar Thapa, a licensed Civil Sub‑Engineer with over fifteen years of hands‑on experience across residential, commercial and government infrastructure projects. From the first topographic survey to the final handover, I stay close to the site — because good engineering is decided on the ground, not just on paper.
